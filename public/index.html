Settings

Voice input

Off by default, so you can review the transcript first. When on, the transcript is sent 2 seconds after you stop speaking unless you tap Edit or change it. Images always wait for Send, and nothing is sent to a different session.

Speech recognition

Use Termux:API only if the phone’s speech services do not work. Allow microphone access when Android asks. Your speech provider may process audio online; this app stores no audio.

Voice output

Tap Read aloud beside Pi’s reply to listen. Replies never play automatically.

Read-aloud engine

Uses Android text-to-speech. Install a voice in Android’s text-to-speech settings if needed. Your selected speech provider may process the reply online. Code blocks are skipped; the text remains visible. Playback stops when you leave the app, start speaking or switch replies.

Replies & feedback

Shows a notification when a reply finishes while Pi Voice is in the background. Tap it to open that session.

A short chime while the app is open. Reply notifications use Android’s notification sound, which you can change in Android’s settings for Pi Voice.

Light vibrations when recording, sending, refreshing and when a reply arrives. Follows Android’s touch-vibration setting.

Keep Pi available

Checking stay-awake support…

Saved for future Pi starts, including when this app is closed. Uses more battery. Set Termux battery use to Unrestricted / allow background activity. Android sleep and network restrictions can still interrupt a task. The display may sleep; Termux’s wake lock is shared across its sessions.

Termux

Pi runs in Termux in the background; this app starts it and resumes your conversation if it stopped. To see Pi’s terminal, open Activity and tap Open in Termux. In Pi, /voice stop disconnects the app and /voice reconnects it. Phone-only connection; no public server.
